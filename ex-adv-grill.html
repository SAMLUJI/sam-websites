Our story

Started with one drum braai in 2016.

Sipho and Lindiwe started grilling for neighbours on weekends. People kept coming back, so they built a kitchen, then a deck, then a second fire pit.

Everything is still cooked over real wood and charcoal, and the chakalaka is still Lindiwe's mother's recipe.
