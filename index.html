What the words mean

PageOne screen of your website with its own link. Basic puts everything on one page; Advanced splits it into separate pages people can tap between.
GalleryA page made only for photos, shown in a grid. Tap one and it opens full-size. Basic doesn't have this; its photos sit inside the one page.
Web address (domain)What people type to find you. A .co.za is your own name and looks more professional than a free address.
Google Business profileThe box with your hours, photos and reviews that shows up on Google and Google Maps.
SEOSetting up your site so Google understands it, which helps you show up when people search.
30 days of support (Pro)For a month after launch, if something on your site breaks, I fix it, and I help with small technical questions. Redesigns and new features aren't included; I'll quote those separately.
Round of changesI show you the site, you send me a list of everything you want changed, and I fix it all in one go.
